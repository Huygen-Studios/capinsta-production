# Capinsta SEO content plan

## Positioning guardrails

- Describe Capinsta as a browser-based, caption-focused workflow.
- Say “currently free during public beta”; never promise permanent free access.
- Link volatile competitor claims to official sources and show a verification date.
- Omit unknown pricing, watermark, resolution, and quota claims instead of guessing.

## Comparison updates

Review `/compare/capinsta-vs-kapwing`, `/compare/capinsta-vs-veed`, and
`/compare/capinsta-vs-captions-ai` every 90 days and after major provider
announcements. Record the review date and official source URLs in the typed
comparison registry.

## Tutorial topics

- How to correct automatic subtitle timing without making captions feel late
- Active-word highlighting for short-form video
- Choosing readable caption position and safe-area margins
- Styling Hinglish and mixed-language captions
- SRT versus VTT versus burned-in captions
- Caption accessibility: contrast, line length, reading speed, and motion

## Original benchmark ideas

- Compare correction time across raw transcripts and edited captions
- Measure readability at common mobile viewport sizes
- Test caption preset contrast against varied video backgrounds
- Publish reproducible word-timing and reading-speed methodology

## Outreach categories

- Creator education newsletters
- Accessibility and inclusive-media communities
- Video editing educators
- Indie product publications
- Browser media and WebCodecs communities
- University media labs and student creator programs

## Linkable assets

- Stable public caption preset gallery
- Caption readability checklist
- Downloadable timing benchmark dataset
- Public brand and screenshot kit
- Technical articles with reproducible examples
- Changelog entries with stable URLs

## Cadence

- Competitor claims: quarterly
- Product capability pages: after each relevant release
- Preset gallery: automatic from the canonical registry
- Tutorials: at least monthly
- Benchmark research: twice yearly
- Brand screenshots: after material UI changes

Code and content can support outreach, but they do not create authoritative
backlinks by themselves. Outreach and editorial relationships remain a real
marketing activity.
