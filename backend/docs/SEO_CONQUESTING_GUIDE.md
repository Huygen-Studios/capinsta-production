# Huygen Caps SEO Conquesting Guide

## Approved competitor page rules

- Use comparison and alternative language only.
- Do not impersonate competitors.
- Do not imply affiliation, partnership, endorsement, sponsorship or official status.
- Do not copy competitor text, layouts, screenshots or logos.
- Prefer plain text competitor names.
- Keep all comparison claims editable in `frontend/src/config/competitors.ts`.
- Mark unverified claims as "Verify before publishing".

## Claims to verify before publishing

- Competitor pricing, plan names and monthly limits.
- Competitor watermark rules.
- Competitor export formats such as SRT, VTT or burned-in video.
- Competitor language support.
- Huygen Caps production export formats and billing rules.
- Huygen Caps storage, rollover and cancellation behavior.

## Trademark disclaimer requirement

Every competitor page must include this style of disclaimer near the hero and final CTA:

`Huygen Caps is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by [Competitor]. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.`

## No fake reviews rule

- Do not add fake ratings.
- Do not add fake testimonials.
- Do not add fake customer logos.
- Do not add fake aggregateRating schema.
- Do not claim awards, users or market share unless verified.

## No fake affiliation rule

Avoid page titles, headings or copy that could imply the page is official. Do not use phrases like "official VEED", "Kapwing login", "free VEED", "Adobe Express account" or competitor support language.

## Page checklist

- One H1.
- Unique title and meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Breadcrumb JSON-LD.
- FAQ section and FAQPage JSON-LD.
- Disclaimer near top and bottom.
- Neutral feature table.
- Internal links to generic Huygen Caps SEO pages.
- No fake claims.
- No copied competitor content.
- No keyword stuffing.

## Sitemap checklist

- Add only useful, non-placeholder competitor pages.
- Do not add "coming soon" pages.
- Keep private app, editor, upload, export and account routes out of the sitemap.
- Confirm `/sitemap.xml` includes each live alternative page.

## Search Console tracking checklist

- Track impressions and clicks for "auto subtitle generator".
- Track impressions and clicks for "AI caption generator".
- Track competitor alternative keywords such as "VEED alternative" and "Kapwing alternative".
- Compare caption-keyword and subtitle-keyword CTR after indexing.
- Expand into `/compare/` pages only after Huygen Caps has verified feature proof.
